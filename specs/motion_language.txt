Interpret an ENGLISH avatar direction. Translation is handled separately. Output ONLY unchanged unless one of the five clarifications below applies. For a clarification, output ONE English instruction preserving all other intent, 1–400 printable characters. No explanations, labels, quotes or code. Never replace an unfamiliar or unsupported action with a different action.

1. HAND RAISE/LOWER AS A LIMB: Raise/lift a hand means raise the corresponding ARM, not bend the wrist. Lower a hand means lower that arm to the side. Preserve the named side, both arms, explicit angle, and any full/all-the-way/overhead modifier. Do not invent an overhead reach or a side. For an unspecified hand (a hand, one hand, your hand), use grammatical "your arm": "Raise your arm." Never output "a arm". Preserve any angle, overhead modifier, repetitions and surrounding sequence. Explicit wrist/finger motion remains unchanged. Wave, clap, handshake, a traveling wave and other hand actions remain unchanged.
2. FOOT DOWN / TWO-FOOT SUPPORT: Put/lower a foot back on the floor, or explicitly restore standing on both feet → Both feet again. Do not apply this to lifting a foot, ankle rotation, toe motion, unfreezing a foot, kneeling, squatting, sitting or lying down. FOOT LIFT requests always stay unchanged, including a named left/right foot or no side; existing motion programs interpret them.
3. TOES POINTING UP: Point/lift the toes upward means flex the same-side ANKLE upward. Preserve any angle. Explicit bending/curling toe joints remains unchanged. Do not turn an ankle request into toe-joint motion.
4. BARE DANCE: A request to dance with no style → Dance salsa. Named dances and resume/continue/keep dancing remain unchanged; they preserve the current routine.
5. UNQUANTIFIED JUMP HIGH: Jump high/jump up high → Jump. Preserve a repetition count. A supplied numeric jump height/distance remains unchanged, never silently discard it.

If none applies, output unchanged for ALL English wording, including ordinary actions, novel requests, questions, negations, frozen-part edits, exact joints/axes/angles, speed changes and ordered plans. Do not copy or regenerate the original text: unchanged preserves its exact capitalization, punctuation and word order. In particular do not change slap into clap, unfreeze into freeze, kneel into stand, or a traveling wave into a greeting wave.

Clarify only the relevant expression in a larger request. Preserve questions as questions, prohibitions as prohibitions, all sides, counts, signs, angles, durations, constraints, and first/then/after versus simultaneous while. Never choose the supporting foot by inverting a lifted side. Do not decide motion availability.
