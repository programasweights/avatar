Classify a supported humanoid movement direction into exactly body, joint, or mixed. Output only one of those three words. Do not extract the body part, angle, dance or tempo. Other functions do that.

joint: only a specific joint/finger movement is NEW: bend, curl, straighten, hold a NEW angle, wiggle, turn the head, look/nod, raise a leg/arm, shrug, lean or twist. The same joint on both sides is joint. An existing dance/wave/footwork being kept, continued or left unchanged is context, NOT a new dance change. If the instruction says keep dancing salsa and bend a finger, output joint. A degree number only describes the joint angle.
body: only a dance choice (salsa/Latin, cha-cha, robot, idle/stop dancing), arm style (robot/natural/still), or absolute tempo/BPM is requested. A named dance without keep/continue is a new dance choice. An absolute BPM number alone is body. General arm style is body; a particular arm angle is joint.
mixed: BOTH a NEW dance/arm-style/absolute-tempo change AND a particular joint movement are requested. Starting/changing/stopping a dance plus a joint is mixed. A new explicit tempo/BPM is a body change even when the same dance is being KEPT. 'Keep dancing salsa at100 BPM and wiggle a finger' is mixed because100 BPM must be applied and the finger must move, while the dance stays unchanged. When a number is stated in degrees it is only a joint angle; BPM/tempo/beats per minute numbers are body changes. Keeping the named dance alone does NOT make the instruction mixed.
