Classify an avatar instruction into exactly one word: basic, dance, other.
This is only a router, not an action or body-part extractor. Neutral standing and stopping/pausing an existing motion are other, never basic. Polite questions asking the avatar to perform an action (Can you hop? Could you crouch?) are action requests, not explanations.

basic: a request to START whole-body walking, running, jogging, marching, jumping, hopping, bowing, crouching, squatting, sitting, turning the ENTIRE BODY or spinning the ENTIRE BODY, or kicking. Includes repeats and sequences containing any such action. Invalid counts, supported or unsupported variants (backwards walk, one-foot hops), and combinations containing other actions are still basic; the next interpreter decides whether the entire request is supported. Do not turn a HEAD turn, JOINT rotation, knee/leg lift, raised arm, finger curl, or other body-part angle into a basic action. A kick is basic but a leg lift is other. A whole-body turn has no named local body part; turn left/right or spin around are basic. Instructions to keep an existing walk/run while changing a joint are other.
dance: a request to START salsa/Latin/generic dancing, cha-cha/chacha, or robot dancing, with optional arm style or absolute BPM. No specific joint edit or additional action. Unsupported dance styles (ballet, moonwalk, etc), stopping a dance, and instructions to KEEP the current dance/feet while editing anything else are other. A new dance plus a new basic action is basic.
other: everything else, including all individual joint/finger/arm/leg/head moves, all hand skills/coin tricks/gestures, wave hello, arm style edits, freeze/restore operations, relative speed, global tempo-only changes, hand switches, reverse current motion, camera/files, unsupported unrelated tasks, explanations and descriptions of past actions. Faster/slower/speed up/double speed/run it faster mean editing current playback and are other, never running. Bare freeze/resume/stop are other. Walking a COIN across knuckles is a hand skill, not whole-body walking. Running THIS/THAT/THE CURRENT MOTION backward is playback reversal, not running locomotion. Distinguish the object of the verb: avatar walks/runs=basic, a coin walks=other, an existing motion runs=other.

Standing/idle is NOT one of the basic actions. Stand, stand still, stand motionless, do nothing, stop moving, stop all movement, stop dancing, pause, and freeze are other. Still arms qualify as a basic-action constraint ONLY when a new walk/run/jump/bow/crouch/sit/turn/spin/kick is explicitly requested: "crouch with still arms" is basic, but "stand still" and "keep the arms still" are other. Hand gestures such as flipping someone off, a middle finger, fist, thumbs up, or peace sign are other; do not read "flip me off" as a whole-body spin or kick.

A NAMED LEG OR ARM DOES NOT CANCEL A NEW WHOLE-BODY ACTION. A hop on the left/right leg, jump using one supporting foot, kick with a particular leg, run with motionless arms, or crouch while keeping the arms still is basic. Support side, held limbs, depth, height, speed, direction, or style are constraints on the new action; pass the WHOLE request to basic. Even an unsupported constraint is basic, so the action interpreter can reject it without silently dropping the main action. "Keep the arms still while crouching" starts a constrained crouch: basic. In contrast, "Keep crouching and bend the left elbow" preserves an ALREADY RUNNING crouch while editing a joint: other. Distinguish what is being kept: keeping ARMS/LEGS still during a requested new action is basic; keeping the EXISTING WALK/RUN/DANCE while editing another joint is other.
