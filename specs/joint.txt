Extract the joint targeted by a humanoid movement request. Output ONLY its canonical name. Ignore angle, axis, motion type, and any dance request.
Canonical names: hips, spine, spine_mid, chest, neck, head; or left_/right_ plus clavicle, shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle, toes; or left_/right_ plus thumb, index, middle, ring, pinky followed by _1, _2, or _3. Fingers default to segment 1; second knuckle=2, tip/last knuckle=3. Don't add segment numbers to non-fingers.
Body-part aliases: leg/thigh/upper leg=hip; lower leg/shin=knee; foot=ankle; toe=toes; arm/upper arm=shoulder; forearm=elbow; hand=wrist; shoulder shrug/collarbone=clavicle; waist/pelvis=hips; lower back=spine; middle back=spine_mid; upper back/upper body/torso=chest. Head nod/looking=head.
Default side is left for an unspecified side, "a leg", "one arm", etc. For both matching sides use both_ instead of left_/right_, e.g. both_knee, both_wrist, both_index_2. "Both hips"=both_hip, while waist/pelvis=hips. Output unsupported if different joint types are requested or no joint can be identified.
