Extract the NEW joint movement from one avatar direction. Return ONLY four space-separated fields:
TARGET MODE OPERATION ANGLE
Return unsupported if no new joint movement is requested, or different kinds of joints/actions are requested. Ignore any existing dance, footwork, wave, or other movement that is being kept. A dance or tempo change in the same sentence is handled separately; extract ONLY the requested joint movement. Never let the dance name choose the finger segment or change a fixed bend into a wiggle.

TARGET is one canonical joint:
hips, spine, spine_mid, chest, neck, head;
left_ or right_ plus clavicle, shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle, toes;
left_ or right_ plus thumb_1, thumb_2, thumb_3, index_1, index_2, index_3, middle_1, middle_2, middle_3, ring_1, ring_2, ring_3, pinky_1, pinky_2, pinky_3.
For the SAME joint on both matching sides use both_ (both_knee, both_shoulder, both_index_1). Use left when no side is specified. Fingers ALWAYS default to segment 1. Only an explicit second/middle knuckle requests 2; explicit tip/last/distal knuckle requests 3. A degree number is never a knuckle index. A whole finger movement selects its base, not all segments.
Aliases: thumb is a finger even without the word finger; little finger=pinky; pointer=index. Leg/thigh/upper leg=hip; lower leg/shin=knee; foot=ankle; toe=toes; arm/upper arm=shoulder; forearm=elbow; hand=wrist; shrug/collarbone=clavicle; waist/pelvis=hips; lower back=spine; middle back=spine_mid; upper back/torso/upper body=chest; looking/nodding=head. Both hips means both_hip, whereas pelvis means hips.

MODE: hold for a new fixed position (bend, curl, lift, turn, hold at an angle, straighten, or generic move). wave ONLY when the requested joint itself should wiggle, oscillate, swing repeatedly, nod or shake. Keeping a wave or a dance elsewhere does NOT make a new bend oscillate. Holding or freezing at a NEW stated angle is hold; pausing at its current pose or restoring saved motion is unsupported.

OPERATION: x, y or z when an explicit axis is given. Otherwise bend, raise, overhead, left, right, backward, forward, outward, inward, up, down.
Finger curls and elbow/knee flexion=bend. Generic move=bend. Straighten or relax a joint=bend with 0 degrees.
Raise/lift an arm or shrug a collarbone=raise. Lift a leg without a direction=raise (a forward lift).
For a complete straight-arm reach above the head, use shoulder hold overhead 180. This means fully lifting an arm, lifting it all the way up, reaching overhead, holding it straight up, raising it as high as possible, or putting it above your head. It sets the whole selected arm overhead with a straight elbow while preserving other movements. Ordinary unspecified arm raises still use raise 45. Overhead is only for fixed arm positions, never a collarbone shrug, leg lift, head movement, or oscillation. An explicit numeric angle or axis always takes priority over words such as fully or high: a stated 45-degree arm raise is raise 45, not overhead 180.
Explicit forward/backward leg or arm movement=forward/backward. Away from the body or sideways=outward; toward/across the body=inward. Raise an arm sideways still uses raise.
Head turning/twisting=left/right (default left). Leaning/bowing=forward/backward. Side tilts use z, negative degrees for left and positive for right. Looking or pointing toes up/down=up/down. Nod=forward. Shake head no=left. An explicit axis overrides anatomical operations.
ANGLE: numeric degrees, preserving signs only for an explicit axis or z tilt. Otherwise use a positive magnitude. Unspecified angle=45, except nod/shake head=20. Straighten=0. Ignore BPM and speed numbers. Supported angle range -180 through 180. No other output fields or explanations.
