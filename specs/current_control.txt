Choose how to interpret ONE avatar instruction. Return exactly one permitted output. Do not explain. This function has no conversation history; contextual commands name an operation on the current scene rather than guessing its action.

Permitted outputs:
body
joint
mixed
dexterity
freeze
restore
unsupported
reverse current
hand left
hand right
hand other
tempo_scale FACTOR
wave left
wave right

joint: head looking up/down/left/right, nodding yes, shaking the head no, or a new angle, bend, straighten, wiggle, turn, lift or generic movement of one named joint/finger, or the same joint on both sides. A thumb is a finger. Polite requests such as "could you move just your thumb" are NEW movement, never freeze. If an explicit new BPM/tempo also appears, use mixed even with keep/continue. Otherwise preserving an existing dance/wave/rest does not request a new dance: "keep dancing salsa and wiggle a finger" is joint.
body: start/change salsa, Latin (=salsa), cha-cha, robot or idle; stop dancing, stand still, stop all movement, or resume a named dance; change arm style to natural, robot, wave or still; or set an explicit absolute tempo/BPM. Unsupported dances are unsupported.
mixed: actually START, CHANGE or STOP a supported dance, or SET an absolute tempo, AND request a specific joint movement. Merely keeping/continuing a named dance is NOT a new dance. A requested dance change plus joint movement is mixed even if the joint has no angle.
dexterity: one finger ripple across one hand; sequential thumb-to-fingertip touches; a traveling wave through both arms; or rolling a coin across one hand's knuckles. "Roll a coin", "do a coin roll", "show the coin trick" and requests to demonstrate a coin roll default to that knuckle trick. "Show me" a supported action means perform it, not explain it. Named side and reversed order are allowed. Preserving existing footwork/dance is allowed. Rolling the coin on a head, face, nose, arm, leg, table or another surface is unsupported; do not drop the object or requested location. Coin flipping/tossing/spinning/catching/balancing is unsupported. Simultaneous tricks in both hands or multiple different skills are unsupported.
freeze: pause a named/selected joint or whole finger at its CURRENT pose. It must actually ask to stop/pause/freeze/disable/keep still. "Move only X" is joint. Holding a NEW angle is joint. Bare Freeze refers to the selected part. Stopping dancing, standing still or stopping the whole character is body, never freeze. Bare pause and camera/playback controls are unsupported. Freezing at a NEW angle is joint.
restore: restore saved motion of a named/selected joint/finger, including let it move AGAIN, resume, unfreeze, or re-enable. A new movement without "again" is joint. Resuming a named dance is body. Bare Restore/Resume/Unfreeze refers to the selected part.
wave left/right: an ordinary greeting, waving hello or goodbye with one hand. Use the named side; default right when no side is given. Preserving the current dance is allowed. This is not a traveling arm wave.
reverse current: reverse the current motion, without specifying a new named skill. "Reverse the left finger ripple" is dexterity.
hand left/right/other: perform the CURRENT action with the explicitly named hand or opposite hand. Do not guess which action is current. A fully named new skill goes to dexterity instead.
tempo_scale FACTOR: relative whole-motion speed, where FACTOR multiplies current speed. Faster/speed up=1.25; slower/slow down=0.8; twice/double speed=2; half speed=0.5; three times speed=3. Preserve an explicitly requested numeric multiplier, from 0.25 to 4. Requests outside that range are unsupported. Absolute BPM is body.
unsupported: all other requests, including new authored gestures such as fist, thumbs up, peace sign, middle-finger gesture, clap, moonwalk, ballet, acrobatics; facial expression; arbitrary prop tricks; explanations; or multiple unrelated requested changes. Do not reduce a gesture or prop interaction to one related joint movement.
