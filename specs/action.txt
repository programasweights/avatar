Translate a basic whole-body action instruction into an ordered program. Output ONLY lines in this exact form:
action NAME COUNT [SUPPORT]
Optionally append one final line: arms still

NAME is one of walk, run, jump, bow, crouch, sit, kneel, lie_down, turn_left, turn_right, spin, kick_left, kick_right, side_kick_left, side_kick_right. COUNT is an integer from 1 through 8, default 1. All counts 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8 are supported, written as digits or words. SUPPORT is optional and allowed ONLY for jump: left, right, or both. Omitted SUPPORT means two-foot jumping. Preserve every requested support foot and arm constraint. Output unsupported if the entire instruction cannot be represented. Do not silently omit any requested action.

Read the complete request before producing output. Check posture compatibility FIRST: lie_down means lying ON THE BACK, never on the stomach, belly, face down, prone, or on either side. Those other orientations are unsupported, even though they contain the word lie. Kneel means BOTH knees; a request for one named knee is unsupported. The words both knees or two knees identify the support anatomy and NEVER mean repeat twice: go onto both knees is action kneel 1. Only an action count such as kneel twice changes COUNT.

For multiple actions output chronological order, not word order. X after Y means Y FIRST and X SECOND. X before Y means X FIRST and Y SECOND. Bow after jumping three times must be action jump 3 followed by action bow 1. Preserve this order even if the first-mentioned verb is bow.

For every kick, choose its trajectory and leg independently. No trajectory or front/forward = kick; side/sideways/lateral = side_kick. No named leg ALWAYS defaults to right for either trajectory. Thus the entire bare instruction side kick is action side_kick_right 1. Keep the default right when adding still hands/arms or a repetition count.

Walk/stroll/march means walk; run/jog/sprint means run, performed in place. Jump/hop/leap defaults to jump (two feet). On the left/right foot or leg means jump with SUPPORT left/right. On one foot/one leg without a named side defaults to SUPPORT left. Each jump step can have its own support foot. Bow/take a bow means bow. Squat/duck/crouch means crouch, lowering and returning upright. Sit/sit down means sit, holding a low seated pose with no chair. An explicitly left/right turn means a 90-degree quarter-turn of the entire body: turn_left/turn_right. A directionless turn, full turn, turn around, or spin means spin, one complete 360-degree turn. For the directionless full-turn default, use spin rather than inventing a left/right direction. This default also applies inside sequences: turn and sit means spin then sit. Kick with left/right leg means kick_left/kick_right; unspecified kick means kick_right. FRONT/FORWARD and SIDE/SIDEWAYS describe different kick trajectories. FRONT RIGHT means a forward kick using the right leg: kick_right, NEVER side_kick_right. The SIDE trajectory requires side, sideways, laterally or out to the side; the word right alone only selects the leg. A spinning/roundhouse/backward kick is ONE unsupported specialized kick, not a spin followed by an ordinary kick. Reverse/backward walking, balances, punches, clapping, emotional acting, acrobatics, new dances, props and simultaneous combinations are unsupported.

QUANTIFIED WHOLE-BODY TURNS: turn_left and turn_right each make 90 degrees. Convert an explicitly requested total angle into quarter-turn COUNT: 90=1, 180=2, 270=3, 360=4, 450=5, 540=6, 630=7, 720=8. A quarter-turn=90 and a half-turn=180. Left/counterclockwise means turn_left; right/clockwise means turn_right. If a specified angle or half-turn has no direction, use turn_left. Thus turn around 180 degrees or face the opposite direction is action turn_left 2. A directionless complete/full 360-degree turn may use action spin 1; two full rotations may use action spin 2. Retain explicit direction even for 360 degrees. "Turn right twice" means two 90-degree turns, COUNT 2. "Turn 180 degrees right twice" means four quarter-turns, COUNT 4. Degrees are the angle of EACH requested turn; multiply quarter-turn count by explicit repetitions, keeping the 1–8 count limit. Other angles (such as 30, 45, 100, negative or zero) are unsupported, never rounded or discarded. Head/neck/hips/shoulder/wrist/finger turns are local joint edits, not these whole-body actions, and are unsupported here. Turning the entire body or rotating yourself does NOT mean turning the head.

A single action defaults to count 1. Once/one=1, twice/two=2, three=3, four=4, five=5, six=6, seven=7, eight=8. Preserve explicit repeats in the COUNT field. Repeating ONE action produces ONE output line, never one line per repetition: five jumps means action jump 5, not five steps. Consecutive actions joined by then, and, comma, followed by, or after that are separate lines in requested order. There are two separate limits: at most 4 action LINES (steps), and at most 16 total REPETITIONS (the sum of their COUNT values), plus the optional final arms still line. The four-line limit does not limit COUNT to four. One line with COUNT 5, 6, 7, or 8 is valid; two lines with COUNT 8 each total 16 and are valid. Only repetitions of an entire MULTI-ACTION sequence expand into ordered lines, if within both limits. Do not confuse repetitions with degrees or speed. Unsupported numbers, zero/negative counts, forever, time durations and incomplete instructions are unsupported. Requests to change an existing motion, body-part edits, explanations, questions about meaning, and descriptions of past actions are unsupported. A polite request to perform a supported action is allowed.


A request for still arms, no arm swing, arms at the sides, without moving arms, WITHOUT MOVING HANDS, or keeping both arms/hands motionless means append arms still after ALL action lines. In ordinary body-action requests hands and arms both mean suppress arm swing. Kick without moving hands is fully supported. This policy applies to the entire requested sequence. It removes active arm articulation while the arms follow the torso. Never silently omit this constraint. Only an EXPLICIT world-space requirement such as hold the hands at these fixed coordinates, a specific held hand pose, still legs during jumping, freezing other body parts, or different arm policies in separate steps is unsupported. Do not add arms still when no arm constraint is requested. Walking/running while waving conflicts with arms still and is unsupported. Starting a new body action with still arms is supported, not a contextual joint edit.

Chair/bed/other furniture, kneeling on a specifically named single knee, and lying on a named side/stomach are unsupported.
Side kick/kick sideways/kick out to the side means side_kick_left or side_kick_right, extending laterally; preserve the named kicking leg, default right. A plain/front/forward kick remains kick_left or kick_right. A left kick without a lateral direction remains kick_left. Do not confuse which leg with which direction. Back kicks, spinning kicks and roundhouse kicks are unsupported.
