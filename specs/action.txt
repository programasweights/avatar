Translate a basic whole-body action instruction into an ordered program. Output ONLY lines in this exact form:
action NAME COUNT [SUPPORT]
Optionally append one final line: arms still

NAME is one of walk, run, jump, bow, crouch, sit, turn_left, turn_right, spin, kick_left, kick_right. COUNT is an integer from 1 through 8, default 1. All counts 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8 are supported, written as digits or words. SUPPORT is optional and allowed ONLY for jump: left, right, or both. Omitted SUPPORT means two-foot jumping. Preserve every requested support foot and arm constraint. Output unsupported if the entire instruction cannot be represented. Do not silently omit any requested action.

Walk/stroll/march means walk; run/jog/sprint means run, performed in place. Jump/hop/leap defaults to jump (two feet). On the left/right foot or leg means jump with SUPPORT left/right. On one foot/one leg without a named side defaults to SUPPORT left. Each jump step can have its own support foot. Bow/take a bow means bow. Squat/duck/crouch means crouch, lowering and returning upright. Sit/sit down means sit, holding a low seated pose with no chair. An explicitly left/right turn means a 90-degree quarter-turn of the entire body: turn_left/turn_right. A directionless turn, full turn, turn around, or spin means spin, one complete 360-degree turn. Do not invent a left/right direction. This default also applies inside sequences: turn and sit means spin then sit. Kick with left/right leg means kick_left/kick_right; unspecified kick means kick_right. Reverse/backward walking, balances, punches, clapping, emotional acting, acrobatics, new dances, props and simultaneous combinations are unsupported.

A single action defaults to count 1. Once/one=1, twice/two=2, three=3, four=4, five=5, six=6, seven=7, eight=8. Preserve explicit repeats in the COUNT field. Repeating ONE action produces ONE output line, never one line per repetition: five jumps means action jump 5, not five steps. Consecutive actions joined by then, and, comma, followed by, or after that are separate lines in requested order. There are two separate limits: at most 4 action LINES (steps), and at most 16 total REPETITIONS (the sum of their COUNT values), plus the optional final arms still line. The four-line limit does not limit COUNT to four. One line with COUNT 5, 6, 7, or 8 is valid; two lines with COUNT 8 each total 16 and are valid. Only repetitions of an entire MULTI-ACTION sequence expand into ordered lines, if within both limits. Do not confuse repetitions with degrees or speed. Unsupported numbers, zero/negative counts, forever, time durations and incomplete instructions are unsupported. Requests to change an existing motion, body-part edits, explanations, questions about meaning, and descriptions of past actions are unsupported. A polite request to perform a supported action is allowed.


A request for still arms, no arm swing, arms at the sides, without moving arms, or keeping both arms motionless means append arms still after ALL action lines. This policy applies to the entire requested sequence. It removes active arm articulation while the arms follow the torso. Never silently omit this constraint. Requests for fixed world-space hands, a specific held hand pose, still legs during jumping, freezing other body parts, or different arm policies in separate steps are unsupported. Do not add arms still when no arm constraint is requested. Walking/running while waving conflicts with arms still and is unsupported. Starting a new body action with still arms is supported, not a contextual joint edit.
