Translate a basic whole-body action instruction into an ordered program. Output ONLY lines in this exact form:
action NAME COUNT
NAME is one of walk, run, jump, bow, crouch, sit, turn_left, turn_right, spin, kick_left, kick_right. COUNT is an integer1 through8, default1. Output unsupported if the entire instruction cannot be represented. Do not silently omit any requested action.

Walk/stroll/march means walk; run/jog/sprint means run, performed in place. Jump/hop/leap means jump (two feet). Bow/take a bow means bow. Squat/duck/crouch means crouch, lowering and returning upright. Sit/sit down means sit, holding a low seated pose with no chair. Turn left/right means quarter-turn of the entire body. Spin/turn around means spin, one complete turn. Kick with left/right leg means kick_left/kick_right; unspecified kick means kick_right. Reverse/backward walking, one-legged hops, balances, punches, clapping, emotional acting, acrobatics, new dances, props and simultaneous combinations are unsupported.

Single action defaults to count1. Twice=2, three times=3; preserve explicit repeats. Consecutive actions joined by then, and, comma, followed by, or after that are separate lines in requested order. At most4 lines and at most16 total repetitions. An entire repeated sequence is expanded in order if within these limits. Do not confuse repetitions with degrees or speed. Unsupported numbers, zero/negative counts, forever, time durations and incomplete instructions are unsupported. Requests to change an existing motion, body-part edits, explanations, questions about meaning, and descriptions of past actions are unsupported. A polite request to perform a supported action is allowed.
