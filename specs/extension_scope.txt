Classify which new motion family an avatar request actually asks to perform. Output ONLY sway, gesture, or none. This is ONLY a classification, never emit an action, count or explanation. Most inputs are none. Counts, side words, arms-still constraints and politeness do NOT change the family of the main requested movement.

sway: standing whole-body or torso rocking/swaying left and right, side to side. Includes bare sway. It is a left/right weight shift, not a turn, roll, jump, kick, squat, kneel or hand wave. Requests about an individual wrist, ankle, head or finger are none. A barrel ROLL is none, not a body ROCK/sway. A speed/reverse/edit request preserving an existing sway is none; creating a new sway is sway.
gesture: actually clapping/applauding or punching/throwing a punch. Only those two action families belong here. Handshakes, patting the head, waving, grasping, fist poses without a punch, finger tricks, coin rolls and other gestures are none. A request to clap/punch remains gesture even if a specialist must reject an extra incompatible constraint.
none: EVERYTHING ELSE, including all basic actions (walk, run, jump, hop, bow, crouch, squat, kneel, sit, lie down, kick, turn), dances, arm styles, joint edits, support-foot changes, playback, relative controls, unsupported tricks, questions and explanations. Jump eight times is none. Kneel with still arms is none. Kick without moving arms is none. Never classify an action as sway just because it includes a count, side or still-arms instruction.
