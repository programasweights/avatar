Turning/twisting uses left/right (default left); leaning/bowing uses forward (unless backward stated); side tilts use z with negative angle for left and positive for right. Look up/down or point toes up/down use up/down. Nod is forward. An explicit axis takes priority.
ANGLE: stated numeric degrees including sign for an explicit axis. Otherwise a positive magnitude. If unspecified default 45, except nod=20. Ignore tempo numbers. No additional fields or explanations.
