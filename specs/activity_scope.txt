Classify an avatar instruction into exactly one word: basic, dance, other.
This is only a router, not an action or body-part extractor.

basic: a request to START whole-body walking, running, jogging, marching, jumping, hopping, bowing, crouching, squatting, sitting, turning the ENTIRE BODY or spinning the ENTIRE BODY, or kicking. Includes repeats and sequences containing any such action. Invalid counts, unsupported variants (backwards walk, one-foot hops), and combinations containing other actions are still basic; the next interpreter decides whether the entire request is supported. Do not turn a HEAD turn, JOINT rotation, knee/leg lift, raised arm, finger curl, or other body-part angle into a basic action. A kick is basic but a leg lift is other. A whole-body turn has no named local body part; turn left/right or spin around are basic. Instructions to keep an existing walk/run while changing a joint are other.
dance: a request to START salsa/Latin/generic dancing, cha-cha/chacha, or robot dancing, with optional arm style or absolute BPM. No specific joint edit or additional action. Unsupported dance styles (ballet, moonwalk, etc), stopping a dance, and instructions to KEEP the current dance/feet while editing anything else are other. A new dance plus a new basic action is basic.
other: everything else, including all individual joint/finger/arm/leg/head moves, all hand skills/coin tricks/gestures, wave hello, arm style edits, freeze/restore operations, relative speed, global tempo-only changes, hand switches, reverse current motion, camera/files, unsupported unrelated tasks, explanations and descriptions of past actions. Faster/slower/speed up/double speed/run it faster mean editing current playback and are other, never running. Bare freeze/resume/stop are other.

An angle does not imply a head or joint. A turn/rotate instruction with NO named local body part, or with whole body/entire body/yourself/face the other way, is basic even when an angle is explicit. A quarter-turn, half-turn, turn around 180 degrees, rotate yourself 270 degrees, and face the opposite direction all turn the ENTIRE BODY. Clockwise/counterclockwise also describe whole-body direction when no local joint is named. Explicit head/neck/hips/wrist/arm/leg rotations remain other, including 90 or 180 degrees. All unsupported whole-body angles remain basic so the action interpreter can reject them instead of moving the head. A request to keep dancing while rotating a specific joint is other.
