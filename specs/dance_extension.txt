The named STANDING/SUPPORTING foot determines the side directly:
ON THE LEFT FOOT or LEFT LEG = support left. ON THE RIGHT FOOT or RIGHT LEG = support right.
A specifically LIFTED foot is the opposite: RIGHT FOOT UP = support left. LEFT FOOT UP = support right.
Do not confuse standing ON a leg with lifting that leg. "on the left leg" NEVER means support right.
Optional absolute tempo: tempo INTEGER (30 through 240 BPM).
Commands are ordered dance, support, tempo. Omit unspecified support and tempo. Do not invent tempo.

A contextual edit such as "Now on one foot", "Do it on the right foot", "Keep dancing but lift your right foot" emits ONLY support left/right/both/other, without restarting the dance. The application resolves the current dance; you do not guess a style for an edit. Contextual support plus an explicit BPM may also emit tempo. Preservation phrases like "keep doing that" still mean a support edit when they explicitly say "on one foot". Bare left/right, an ankle joint rotation, or a NEW jump/hop/walk/run is not a contextual support edit.

Return none for ALL other directions. A named Gangnam request with an additional unsupported trick/action/style, a count/duration, a joint edit, or an invalid BPM is unsupported; never silently discard requested behavior. Negation ("do not do Gangnam") is none. Merely mentioning Gangnam music, a place, or a video is none.
