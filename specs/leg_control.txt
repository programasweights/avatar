FIRST require the input to explicitly NAME the whole leg(s), lower-body motion, both feet, or footwork as the thing being paused/restored. freeze means hold the selected whole leg at its current pose: stop its movement, pause it, freeze it, keep it still, stop the footwork. restore means let a previously paused leg move again, resume that leg or restore the original footwork. A left/right qualifier selects that leg. Legs, leg movements, lower-body motion, feet or footwork without a side means both_legs. Preserve the side and operation. Keeping the current dance or upper body going does not cancel a selective leg pause.

Require BOTH a leg/footwork target AND an explicit pause-or-restore operation. Naming a leg never implies freezing it. A new kick, hop, leg lift, step, swing, bend or movement is none, even when short, imperative, or appearing after an earlier sequence step. "Kick left leg" tells it to KICK, not freeze. Bare "move the leg" requests new movement; only "let the paused leg move again", "resume the leg", or equivalent explicitly restores paused motion. Do not infer a pause or an earlier freeze that the input never mentions.

Support/contact changes are none, even with "keep", "hold", "again" or "back". One foot touching the floor, the other leg raised, both feet again, using both legs again, and returning to two-footed dancing describe support, not paused motion. "Again" alone does NOT mean restore; the instruction must restore/resume leg MOVEMENT or explicitly unfreeze a leg. "Keep the dance going on one foot" keeps an ongoing dance; it never asks to keep that leg still. Hold feet where they currently are means freeze, but place/hold a foot at a NEW height or contact position means none.

Decide the ANATOMICAL TARGET before the operation. A named finger, hand, wrist, elbow, arm, shoulder, head, neck, torso, hip, knee, ankle or toe joint is NONE, even if the request says freeze, stop, resume or restore. A right ring finger is a FINGER, never a right leg. Left/right is only a side qualifier, not evidence of a leg. Only an explicitly requested whole leg, lower body, both feet or footwork belongs here.

Only controls of EXISTING leg motion belong here. Most requests are none. Starting a new jump/kick/run/walk/dance with a fixed-limb constraint is none; it must be handled as a whole new action. Balance or dance on one foot, switch supporting feet, lift a leg, bend a knee, a new stated joint angle, standing, sitting, kneeling, lying down, and new static poses are none. Whole-animation stop/pause/resume and freezing hands/fingers/arms/head are none. Never turn a whole-body stop into a leg pause or a leg pause into a whole-body stop. Questions, descriptions, negated requests and unrelated meanings of foot/leg are none. If other NEW actions or joint edits are requested in the same atomic instruction, output none instead of dropping them.
