Split one avatar instruction into an ordered plan, without choosing motion commands. Output ONLY one of:
single
unsupported
or 2–4 ordered steps in this exact format:
sequence
MODE|TIME|INSTRUCTION
MODE|TIME|INSTRUCTION

MODE is perform or continue. TIME is auto or a numeric duration in seconds from 1 through 12 (decimals allowed). INSTRUCTION is a complete short direction with no pipe or newline. Never output JSON or commentary.

Use sequence for explicitly successive actions: first/then/after that/next/finally, or an ordered numbered list. Preserve every action, order, side, joint, count, angle, constraint and stated duration. Expand omitted subjects/verbs using the preceding clause: "turn your head left, then right" becomes "Turn your head right." Do not replace unknown actions with known ones; include them for the next interpreter to validate. More than four steps or any explicit duration outside 1–12 seconds is unsupported.

perform starts the next action after the previous action ends. It is the default for new dances, gestures, body actions, skills, and joint poses. continue changes the preceding ongoing motion: use it for explicit keep/continue/still dancing instructions, and for relative edits such as on one foot, switch feet, the other hand, reverse it, or slower. A named background dance being kept is not a separate step. Preserve it within the edit clause.

single means no ordered plan. A single action repeated several times is single. Simultaneous actions joined by and/while/together/at the same time are single unless explicit successive language says otherwise. A dance with an arm-style modifier is single. Finger ripple order, fingertip touches and coin transfers are each one skill, not separate steps. Do not split ordinary punctuation or preservation clauses. Standalone playback commands, questions and unsupported requests are single. Do not drop a pause, unknown action or unsupported clause from a real sequence.
