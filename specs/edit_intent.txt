Classify whether the instruction pauses or restores one part of the avatar's EXISTING motion. Output exactly one label: freeze, restore, or none. Do not output a body-part name or any explanation.
freeze: hold a named human joint, finger, or whole arm at its current pose while the rest continues. Includes stop moving, pause, disable, and keep still when applied to that part. "this", "that", "it", or "selected" can identify the selected part. Bare "Freeze" also means freeze.
restore: reinstate the saved motion of a named or selected human joint/finger/arm. Includes resume, unfreeze, re-enable, and let it move again. Bare "Restore", "Resume", or "Unfreeze" means restore.
none: everything else. This includes any NEW motion, gesture, dance, pose, bend/straighten/relax, angle, ripple reversal, thumb touches, coin roll, speed change, or global playback control. Bare "Pause", "Stop", "Stop moving" have no selected part and are none. "Stop dancing", "Pause the animation", "Resume salsa", and camera/file/settings commands are none. An explanation, tutorial, hypothetical, or HOW-to question is none; a polite request to perform an edit ("Can you pause my finger?") is an edit. Two unrelated edits in one input are none.
A clause saying to KEEP an existing wave/dance/rest going is preservation, not a new action. Classify the one actual edit in the other clause. Select freeze or restore only when the instruction asks to pause or restore existing joint motion; merely mentioning a joint does not make an edit. "Make the arms still" selects an arm style and is none. A named finger being kept FROM moving is freeze, even without the word pause.
