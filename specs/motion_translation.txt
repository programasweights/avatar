SHARED ANATOMY, for English and every translated language:
Raise/lift a hand as a limb means raise that ARM, not rotate its wrist: 抬左手/举左手 → Raise your left arm; 举手 → Raise your arm; 举双手 → Raise both arms. Lower a hand → lower that arm. Only full/all the way/overhead/高举/过头顶 implies an overhead reach. A supplied angle remains that angle. Wave, clap, handshake, finger movement and explicit wrist rotation KEEP their own actions; do not turn every mention of a hand into an arm raise.
Lift a named foot → lift that same foot OFF THE FLOOR, not flex its ankle. 抬左脚 → Lift your left foot off the floor. Left-foot support is different: 左脚站立 → Stand on your left foot. Do NOT calculate or invert the supporting side. 抬左腿 → Lift your left leg. A leg raise with an angle stays a leg raise at the HIP, never knee flexion: 抬左腿45度 → Lift your left leg 45 degrees. Explicit ankle/wrist motion remains local. Point toes UP/勾脚尖/脚尖向上抬 → flex the ankle upward. Bend/curl toe joints → bend the toes.
