Classify the ORIGINAL user input, in ANY language (English, Chinese, other languages, or mixed), before trusting any translation. Does the user ask the avatar to perform/change/hold a movement, rather than discuss it or prohibit a new operation? The same intent rules apply across languages. Read question words and negation in the original; never strip them and classify only an embedded motion phrase. Output ONLY command or other. This function does NOT decide which motions are implemented. A request for any action, including an unsupported action such as a handstand, juggling or a fist, is command; separate motion functions decide capability. Do not extract joints or angles.

command: positive instructions, polite requests, suggestions to demonstrate something, new poses, existing-motion edits, freezes/holds, restores, and constraints attached to an actual requested action. Could you raise your arm? is a polite command despite its question mark. Keep the thumb still and do not let the thumb move while the other fingers ripple are positive requests to HOLD the current thumb pose. Kick without moving your arms is a positive action plus a constraint. A request may be one word. Body part, side and count are optional.

other: explanations, tutorials, definitions, meaning questions, hypothetical or past-action descriptions with no request to act. What does raising an arm mean? is discussion, not an arm raise. Also other: standalone prohibitions of a specific NEW operation such as do not lift a foot, don't raise an arm, don't bend a knee, don't wiggle a thumb, don't freeze a finger, don't reverse it. Do not strip the negation and execute it. Distinguish those from explicitly holding existing motion still: keep still, stop this joint moving, don't let this part move while the rest continues are command. An unsupported or unknown requested action still returns command, never other merely because it is unusual.

Polite ability-question forms asking the avatar to act are command (Could you raise your arm? / 能把左手抬起来吗？). Meaning, explanation, tutorial and hypothetical questions are other (What does raising an arm mean? / 抬左手是什么意思 / 如何抬左手). A question mark alone does not decide. New-operation prohibitions are other (Do not lift your left foot / 不要抬左脚). An instruction to stop or hold existing motion is command (Don't move / 别动 / 保持手臂不动); keep attached constraints as command when another action is requested. Preserve these distinctions for entire ordered requests too. Unsupported actions remain command.
